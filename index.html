Proceeds, by period and by app
Data from App Store Connect, refreshed daily by GitHub Actions. Downloads are first-time installs; updates and re-downloads are excluded. They come from App Analytics on UTC days, so they match the App Analytics pages in App Store Connect; the newest two days are still filling in. An app shows Pacific-time days from the daily sales report only until Apple delivers its first App Analytics report. The charts and totals are App Store only. Google Play is reported separately per app, because Apple counts first-time downloads and Play counts user installs — neither contains the other, so a combined figure would have no definition. Play also publishes about a week behind Apple, so each Play line states the last date it actually covers. Proceeds are Apple's estimate from the daily sales reports, not what was paid out. Ad income is reported separately under Payments and never added to them: different accounts, different payment schedules, and a combined total would reconcile against neither statement.

Foreign currencies are converted, and the conversion is checked. Apple reports proceeds in the currency of the region a sale came from, so a Korean purchase arrives as KRW rather than dollars. This page used to show only the dollar rows, which was defensible while foreign sales were rare and badly wrong once they were the majority: on 31 October six of eight purchases were foreign and the figure shown was $5.60 of a roughly $34 day.

Everything is now converted at the European Central Bank's reference rate for the day of the sale, and every converted figure can be taken apart — hover a day on the payments calendar to see the amounts it was built from. Apple settles at its own rate on its own date, so these are estimates, and the Estimate vs Apple table on the Payments tab measures each closed period against the finance report for it rather than assuming the two agree.
