Data from App Store Connect, refreshed daily by GitHub Actions. Downloads are first-time installs; updates and re-downloads are excluded. The charts and totals are App Store only. Google Play is reported separately per app, because Apple counts first-time downloads and Play counts user installs — neither contains the other, so a combined figure would have no definition. Play also publishes about a week behind Apple, so each Play line states the last date it actually covers. Proceeds are Apple's estimate from the daily sales reports, not what was paid out, and exclude AdMob — ad income is a separate account and appears nowhere on this page.

Currencies are listed separately and never added together. Apple reports proceeds in the currency of the region a sale came from, so a Korean purchase arrives as KRW rather than dollars. Converting here would mean picking an exchange rate, and Apple settles at its own rate on the day it pays — so any total this page invented would be a number that could never be reconciled against a bank statement.

To judge scale, compare the same product rather than the currency: Cosmica's Everything Bundle returns $3.50 in the United States and ₩4,900 in Korea. Those are the same sale at the same price tier. Won are small units, which makes a routine purchase look like a large number.
